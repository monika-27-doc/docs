## Get up and running in four steps

New to AetherFlow? Follow this quick onboarding path to automate your first process today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.aetherflow.io](https://app.aetherflow.io) and complete the guided product tour, which walks you through prompts, triggers, integrations, and the workflow builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an application">
    Navigate to **Integrations** and authenticate at least one business application — such as your CRM, helpdesk, or messaging tool — so AetherFlow can read and write data on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a workflow">
    Open the **Workflow Builder**, describe your automation in plain language, and review the AI-generated steps. Adjust conditions or triggers as needed to match your exact requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate and monitor">
    Run a test to confirm the workflow behaves correctly, then activate it for live production use. Watch real-time execution logs and analytics to keep everything running smoothly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a simple, single-step automation to get comfortable with the builder before tackling more complex, multi-app workflows.
</Tip>

AetherFlow transforms plain-language descriptions into fully executable workflows — no coding required. This guide walks you through every stage of building your first automation, from writing your initial prompt to switching the workflow on in production.

<Tip>
  Start with a simple, tightly scoped use case — for example, "send me a Slack message whenever a new row is added to my Google Sheet." Nailing a small workflow first teaches you how AetherFlow interprets your prompts and gives you a solid foundation before you tackle multi-step automations.
</Tip>

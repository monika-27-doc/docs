Integrations are the bridge between AetherFlow and the tools your team already uses every day. By connecting your apps, you give AetherFlow the ability to trigger workflows based on real-world events and take actions — sending messages, updating records, firing notifications — inside the tools that matter most to your business. Start with the apps at the center of your highest-value workflows, then expand your integration library as your automation needs grow.

## How to Connect an App

Follow these steps to authorize and configure any integration in AetherFlow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations area">
    In the AetherFlow sidebar, click **Integrations**. You'll see a searchable catalog of all available apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an application">
    Find the app you want to connect — either by browsing the catalog or using the search bar — then click its tile to open the integration detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the requested permissions">
    Click **Connect**. AetherFlow will redirect you to the app's own authorization screen, where you can review and grant the permissions required for your workflows. Only the permissions you approve are ever used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the connection">
    After authorization, return to AetherFlow to complete the setup. Depending on the app, you may need to select a workspace, server, account, or other scope. Give the connection a recognizable name so you can identify it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the integration in a workflow">
    Your connected app is now available as a trigger or action inside the workflow builder. Describe what you want to automate in plain language and AetherFlow will map it to the right integration automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Best Practices

<Warning>
  **Review permissions carefully before authorizing any integration.** Each app requests a specific set of permissions — grant only what your workflows genuinely need. AetherFlow displays the full permission list on the authorization screen so you can make an informed decision before proceeding.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Remove unused connections.** If you stop using an integration, navigate to **Integrations → Connected Apps**, find the connection, and click **Disconnect**. Removing dormant connections reduces your attack surface and keeps your integration list clean and auditable.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Start with the two or three apps that appear in your most critical workflows. A focused integration library is easier to manage and audit than a large one built up without a clear purpose.
</Tip>

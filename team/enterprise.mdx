## Governance in Practice

Enterprise governance in AetherFlow operates at three levels: **workspace policies**, **workflow-level controls**, and **integration guardrails**.

**Workspace policies** define the rules that apply to every automation in your organization — for example, requiring all production workflows to have an owner, a description, and at least one error notification configured before they can be published.

**Workflow-level controls** apply to individual automations. Use approval workflows to gate changes to your most sensitive automations, and assign a designated owner to every workflow so accountability is never ambiguous.

**Integration guardrails** let your platform administrators pre-approve a list of integrations that are permitted in production workflows. Teams can prototype with any available integration, but only reviewed and approved connections can be used in live, scheduled automations.

## Audit Logs and Compliance Readiness

Audit logs capture a detailed, time-stamped record of every significant event in your AetherFlow workspace. Each log entry records the actor, the action taken, the affected resource, and the timestamp.

Key events captured in the audit log include:

- Workflow created, edited, published, or deleted
- Team member invited, role changed, or removed
- Integration connected or disconnected
- Approval request submitted, approved, or rejected
- Execution log accessed or exported

Audit logs are available for export in JSON and CSV formats from **Settings → Audit Logs**. Enterprise customers can also configure a log streaming destination — such as Splunk, Datadog, or an S3 bucket — for continuous, automated export.

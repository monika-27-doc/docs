AetherFlow is an AI-powered workflow automation platform. Describe your automation in plain language, and AetherFlow assembles the steps, integrations, and logic to execute it as a live, production-ready workflow you can monitor, refine, and share.

## What AetherFlow does

AetherFlow turns a natural-language prompt, for example _"When a new lead is created in our CRM, send a welcome email and add the contact to our marketing list"_, into executable steps. Each step maps to an action in a connected app, with data flowing automatically between systems.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your automation">
    Type a plain-language description in the Workflow Builder. The AI asks clarifying questions when it needs more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the generated workflow">
    AetherFlow renders the workflow as a visual sequence. Adjust steps, data mappings, and conditions as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your applications">
    Authenticate the apps your workflow uses. AetherFlow securely stores credentials and handles API calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, activate, and monitor">
    Validate with real or sample data, activate for live use, and track every run in the monitoring dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who AetherFlow is built for

AetherFlow is built for operations managers, product teams, developers, and anyone automating cross-application processes without writing custom integration code. If you can describe a repetitive task, AetherFlow can automate it.

<Note>
  AetherFlow runs entirely in the browser. All processing, credential storage, and execution happen on AetherFlow's secure cloud infrastructure — nothing to install or maintain.
</Note>

AetherFlow is an AI-powered workflow automation platform that removes the gap between what you want your software to do and the technical effort it normally takes to make it happen. Instead of writing code or configuring rigid rule engines, you simply describe your automation in plain language — AetherFlow interprets your intent and assembles the workflow steps, integrations, and logic required to execute it. The result is a live, production-ready automation that you can monitor, refine, and share with your team.

## What AetherFlow does

At its core, AetherFlow takes a natural-language prompt — for example, *"When a new lead is created in our CRM, send a welcome email and add the contact to our marketing list"* — and translates it into a structured series of executable steps. Each step maps to a real action in a connected application, with the data flowing automatically from one system to the next.

## How it works

AetherFlow follows a consistent four-stage process from your description to a live workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your automation">
    Type a plain-language description of what you want to automate in the Workflow Builder prompt field. Be as specific or as high-level as you like — the AI asks clarifying questions when it needs more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the generated workflow">
    AetherFlow presents the translated workflow as a visual sequence of steps. Inspect each step, adjust data mappings, and add or remove conditions to ensure the logic is exactly right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your applications">
    Authenticate the applications your workflow needs to access. AetherFlow securely stores credentials and handles API communication on your behalf throughout every run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, activate, and monitor">
    Run the workflow against real or sample data to validate behavior. Once you're satisfied, activate it for live use and track every execution through the monitoring dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who AetherFlow is built for

AetherFlow is designed for operations managers, product teams, developers, and anyone who needs to automate cross-application processes without writing and maintaining custom integration code. If you can describe a repetitive task, AetherFlow can automate it.

<Note>
  AetherFlow runs entirely in the browser — there is nothing to install or maintain on your local machine or servers. All processing, credential storage, and execution happen on AetherFlow's secure cloud infrastructure.
</Note>
